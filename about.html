About Us

Passionate about travel, dedicated to you

Our Story

Founded in 2009, Umbrella Tours & Travel was born from a simple passion: to share the incredible beauty of the world with curious travelers. What started as a small team of two travel enthusiasts in Nairobi has grown into a thriving agency serving thousands of clients globally.

Over 15 years, we've curated hundreds of tours across six continents, always staying true to our founding principle — that travel should be transformative, accessible, and stress-free. We believe every journey has the power to change perspective and create lasting memories.

Today, our expert team of guides, planners, and support staff works tirelessly to ensure that every trip we organize exceeds expectations. From safari adventures in Kenya to cherry blossom tours in Japan, we pour the same care and expertise into every itinerary.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional travel experiences that inspire, educate, and connect people with the world's most remarkable destinations. We are committed to delivering personalized, high-quality journeys that create lasting memories and foster a deeper appreciation for diverse cultures and natural wonders.
