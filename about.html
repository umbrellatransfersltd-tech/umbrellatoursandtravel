About Us

London's trusted chauffeur tour operator since 2014

Our Story

Founded in 2014, Umbrella Tours & Travel has been London's trusted chauffeur tour operator for over 10 years. We take visitors from around the world on unforgettable journeys across Britain — from the iconic White Cliffs of Dover to the mystical stones of Stonehenge, the dreaming spires of Oxford to the scenic lakes of Cumbria.

Every journey is private, comfortable, and tailored to you. Our professional chauffeurs are more than drivers — they are knowledgeable guides who bring Britain's history and culture to life during every mile of the journey.

Today, our expert team works tirelessly to ensure that every trip we organise exceeds expectations. Whether you want a half-day royal tour to Windsor or a two-day adventure to the Lake District, we pour the same care and expertise into every itinerary.

Our Mission
