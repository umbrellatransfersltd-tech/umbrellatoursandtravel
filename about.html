Est. London

A London Story Born from a Love of Travel

Umbrella Tours & Travel was founded with one simple but powerful belief: that travel is most meaningful when it is personal, private and beautifully crafted. Based in the heart of London, we set out to offer discerning travellers something the big coach tours could never provide — time, flexibility and a genuine connection with the places they visit.

What began as a small team of passionate guides with a handful of luxury vehicles has grown into one of London’s most respected private tour operators, with thousands of satisfied guests from over 60 countries. Yet our ethos has never changed: every tour we operate is treated as if it were the only one we do that day.

Whether you arrive from New York or Nairobi, whether you want to visit Stonehenge or the streets of Kyoto, our mission is the same — to make your journey extraordinary, seamless and deeply memorable.

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To deliver the finest private chauffeur travel experiences in the UK and worldwide, providing every guest with memories that last a lifetime. We are committed to personal service, expert knowledge and impeccable quality at every step of the journey.

Our Vision

To be recognised globally as the gold standard in private chauffeur travel, setting the benchmark for excellence in bespoke UK tours and international travel experiences. We envision a world where every traveller can access extraordinary journeys tailored uniquely to them.

Our Values

Excellence, integrity, warmth and a genuine passion for travel. We believe that hospitality is not a transaction but a human connection. Every guest is treated as a friend, every tour planned with care, and every experience delivered with pride.
