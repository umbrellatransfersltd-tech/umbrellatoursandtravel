Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking a private chauffeur tour with us.

Simply fill in our contact form above, send us an email or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us your preferred tour, dates, number of guests and any special requirements. We’ll respond within 2 hours with full details and a personalised quote. Once you’re happy, a small deposit secures your booking.
All our tour prices include your private luxury vehicle, a professional chauffeur or guide, hotel pickup and drop-off in London, and all parking and road charges. Entry tickets to attractions are quoted separately (we can arrange these for you). There are no hidden extras — what we quote is what you pay.
We pick up directly from your hotel, serviced apartment or any address in London. Airport pickups from Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted and London City Airport are also available. Just let us know your location when booking and we’ll handle the rest.
Our vehicles accommodate up to 8 guests for standard tours. For groups of up to 16, we can provide a luxury minibus. For larger corporate or group events of 20 or more, we arrange a fleet of vehicles. All group tours are bespoke — simply enquire and we’ll create the perfect solution.
Absolutely — every tour we operate is flexible and bespoke. Want to spend more time at Stonehenge and skip an attraction? Prefer a longer lunch stop in a particular village? Want to combine two of our listed tours into one day? Just let us know. We design the tour around you, not the other way around.
Our tours operate rain or shine — after all, Stonehenge and Windsor Castle are just as magnificent in the mist! For outdoor-heavy tours in extreme weather, we may suggest adapting the itinerary. If you wish to reschedule due to severe weather, we will do our best to accommodate a date change at no extra charge. Please note that attraction closures are outside our control.
We understand that plans change. Cancellations made more than 48 hours before your tour date receive a full refund of any deposit paid. Cancellations within 48 hours may forfeit the deposit. For world tours and multi-day packages, specific cancellation terms apply and will be clearly stated in your booking confirmation.
